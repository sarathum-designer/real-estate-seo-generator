Kendra means "centre" in Hindi and several other Indian languages. The pitch in one line: the GCC ecosystem doesn't lack data, it lacks a resolved, sourced list. We build that list and the products on top of it.

The answer on one page

The data exists. Nobody has resolved it into one list.

PROBLEM

One study counts 2,117 GCCs and 3,728 centres. No report names them. Entrants, providers and states each pay a referral tax to learn what is true.

OPPORTUNITY

An entity-resolved, sourced registry of GCCs is the base layer. Signals, location intelligence and provider track records are all queries on it.

SOLUTION · POSITION

Build the ledger with AI ingestion. Sell signals to providers first; give entrants the truth free. Be the neutral record: no pay-to-rank.

If you read only one slide: the wedge is entity resolution plus provenance, not more reports. Revenue comes first from service providers who need leads every month; trust comes from entrants who get the truth free.

Each report is honest about its method. The problem is that each counts something different: companies vs centres, with or without mid-market, different dates. A new entrant can't tell which number is "true" because the question itself is ambiguous. That is the core of the information asymmetry.

The problem · the players

One entrant, eight counterparties, no shared map

Setup advisors / Big 4

See many mandates. Sell the study, so their view is the product.

Law firms

See entity structures early. Bound by confidentiality.

Auditors and tax

See transfer pricing and real costs. Rarely shared.

Real estate firms

See space requirements months ahead. Paid on the deal.

The entrant

Makes a 5–10 year, multi-million dollar call once, with the least information of anyone.

Recruiters

See talent supply and pay. Paid per hire.

Payroll, EOR, BOT

See early-stage teams before any filing. Want the transfer later.

State governments

See MoUs and incentive claims. Compete with every other state.

Industry bodies, media

Publish aggregates. Don't publish the list.

Each player holds a slice of the truth, and most are paid in ways that make them partial: advisors sell studies, brokers earn on the lease, recruiters per hire. Nobody's incentive is to assemble the whole picture and give it away. That is why the market runs on referrals.

The problem · root cause

Data is not missing. It is unresolved.

Scattered

MCA filings, STPI and SEZ approvals, state MoUs, press, job posts, lease deals.

Unresolved

One GCC shows up under a legal entity, a brand, an acquired unit and a nickname.

Unverified

A provider's "40+ GCCs set up" looks the same as a filed lease.

Stale

Annual reports, in a market adding about four GCCs a week.

This reframing drives everything after it. If the problem were missing data, the answer would be another survey. Because the problem is resolution and provenance, the answer is a ledger: entities, claims, sources, confidence. Modern LLMs make extraction and matching cheap enough to do this at the scale of every filing and press release.

The opportunities · what matters most

The registry comes first, because everything else joins on it

1

It is the base layer

Location intelligence, provider records and signals are all queries on "which GCC, where, with whom". Without resolved entities, each is one more scattered dataset.

2

It has buyers on day one

Thousands of providers need leads every month. About 200 entrants a year need the truth once. Recurring need pays the bills.

3

It compounds

Every new document makes the next match cheaper and the confidence higher. A competitor who starts later starts with less evidence.

Provider delivery records are the most valuable long-term asset, but they can't exist before the registry: a delivery record is a link between a provider and a resolved GCC entity, backed by evidence.

The product surface is deliberately thin. The asset is the ledger underneath. Each product is a view on it for a different stakeholder, which keeps the build small and makes every correction improve every product at once.

The solution · how the ledger is built

AI does the reading and matching. The maths stays transparent.

1 · COLLECT

MCA filings, STPI/SEZ, state MoUs, press, job posts, leases

2 · EXTRACT

LLM turns each document into typed claims with the quote

3 · RESOLVE

Names, brands, aliases merge; low-confidence goes to review

4 · SCORE

Confidence from independent source types and recency

5 · SERVE

Products and the analyst read the same ledger

Where AI is used

Extraction from unstructured text, entity matching, signal detection in job posts, and the analyst agent that answers with tool calls.

Where it is not

Counts, confidence scores and rankings are plain formulas anyone can inspect. An LLM never invents a number that reaches a user.

Confidence = 1 minus the product of (1 minus weight) across independent source types. A filing and a lease together are near-certain; one news report alone is not. Conflicting claims stay visible. The prototype runs steps 2 to 5 live.

The solution · for whom first

Providers pay first. Entrants get the truth free.

FIRST PAYING USER

Service providers: real estate, recruiters, EOR, law, BOT

  • Need leads every week, not once
  • Have budget and short buying cycles
  • Tell us fast when the data is wrong

FIRST TRUSTED AUDIENCE

Entrants and GCC leaders, through a free public index

  • Bring credibility and corrections
  • Make a verified profile worth having
  • Become the demand side of the marketplace later
The tension: if providers pay, will entrants trust us? That's why provider money buys data access and a profile, never a ranking. States come next, 6 to 18 months in, as buyers of benchmarking dashboards.

Phase one is deliberately narrow: six cities, the providers who most need leads, and a public index to build the brand. The index becomes the number journalists and states cite, which is the cheapest marketing there is.

The positioning

The record, not another report.

For anyone making a decision about India's GCCs, Kendra is the evidence ledger that names every GCC and shows the source behind every number. Unlike consultant reports, it is a list you can check. Unlike brokers and advisors, it does not sell the answer it gives.

Positioning against three alternatives: reports (aggregate, annual, unverifiable), advisors and brokers (useful but conflicted), and generic databases like Tracxn or LinkedIn (not GCC-aware, no entity resolution across Indian filings). Kendra is neutral, granular, and current.

The positioning · the moat

Neutrality is the moat, so it is written down

No pay-to-rank, ever

Providers can pay for a profile and data access. Never for a position.

Every number links to evidence

Gaps are shown as gaps. Conflicting claims stay visible.

Anyone can correct

Corrections need evidence. The change log is public.

Open method

Definitions are switchable: companies or centres, with or without mid-market.

The flywheel: corrections from GCCs and providers improve the ledger, a better ledger attracts more entrants, more entrants make a verified profile more valuable to providers, and their evidence improves the ledger again.

Document in; claims, entity match and ledger diff out.

The AI runs three ways: on Claude inside claude.ai using the viewer's account, on an Anthropic API key anywhere else, or on an offline rule engine so the demo never breaks. Ask Kendra is a real tool-using agent: Claude calls search_registry, compare_cities, rank_cities, find_providers and upcoming_gccs, and the answer shows which tools it used. Market figures are real and cited; company, provider and signal records are fictional on purpose, so nothing in the demo is a false claim about a real company.

Incumbents publish their listsTheir lists feed services they sell; neutrality and speed are hard for them to copy
Provider revenue erodes trustWritten charter, ranking separate from revenue, public change log
Data access limitsOfficial filings, state data and licensed feeds; no scraping behind logins
The first risk is the one that matters most. If ten providers won't pay for a hand-built list, the automated version won't save the business. The 30-day sprint is designed to answer that before any serious engineering.

How this was made

AI for research, sparring and building; judgement on top

Research

AI web research across Nasscom–Zinnov, EY, ANSR, ICRA, ANAROCK and state policy notes. Every figure in the deck kept with its source; conflicts kept, not smoothed.

Sparring

Opportunities scored against four tests and argued both ways. The "data is unresolved, not missing" reframe came from noticing the 2,117 vs 3,728 gap.

Building

Prototype built with Claude and run on Claude: extraction to typed claims, and a tool-using analyst. Deterministic maths wherever a number reaches a user.

Fabricated precision is the failure mode of AI-made strategy work. The rule here was simple: anything stated as a fact has a source; anything estimated says so; anything illustrative is labelled.
